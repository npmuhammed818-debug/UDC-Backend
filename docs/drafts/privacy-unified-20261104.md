# UpDownCircle privacy policy - proposed unified text

Draft for owner and lawyer review, 4 November 2026. Not published. Replace both public-site and backend policy text only after approval. Do not describe this draft as legal certification.

## Who we are
UpDownCircle (UDC) helps businesses coordinate international trade through WhatsApp. Muhammed NP operates UDC in Kerala, India. For privacy questions, access, correction, consent withdrawal or deletion requests, contact service@updowncircle.site or the official UDC WhatsApp conversation you already use.

## What we collect and why
We process your name, WhatsApp number, email, business role, company details and country to set up and run your business account. We process trade requirements, listings, offers, deal documents, shipment updates and agreed commission records to support the trade workflow. We process messages and, when you use them, voice notes, images and files to understand and answer your request. We also keep consent records, access records and security/audit logs to protect the service and handle disputes.

Business verification uses company registration/trade licences, tax documents and relevant import/export or product licences. Relevant past-export evidence may be requested from sellers. Bank proof is a separate optional later step where needed for a transaction. Do not send Aadhaar, passport, bank statements, passwords, card credentials or unrelated identity papers. Use the private upload link for sensitive documents, not WhatsApp attachments.

## AI and human review
AKIF uses AI to understand messages, extract trade details, draft replies and assist with document checks. AI can make mistakes. A human reviews verification decisions and sensitive legal, banking, payment and trade commitments. AI output is not proof that a business, document or transaction is genuine.

AI processing may include the relevant message text, conversation context, permitted customer memory, extracted document text, and audio or images needed for your request. Raw verification files are not automatically included in ordinary chat prompts. Sending a file in WhatsApp can expose it to WhatsApp and the document-processing workflow; use the private upload link instead.

## Who processes data
Only the data needed for a feature is sent to the services used for that feature:
- Meta/WhatsApp carries messages and attachments.
- Render hosts the backend and AKIF worker; Supabase stores database records and uploaded documents.
- Groq processes the configured chat models and can transcribe audio. A model name containing OpenAI does not by itself mean data is sent to OpenAI's API.
- Google Gemini is a configured backup for chat and supports enabled speech/image features. Where Google's free-tier terms allow use of submitted content to improve its services, that processing must be considered before using those features. Do not submit sensitive information for those features.
- ElevenLabs provides enabled speech generation/transcription and voice fallback.
- Resend sends enabled account-verification and service emails.
- Anthropic Claude is planned, but not confirmed as active in the reviewed staging configuration. It must be disclosed as active before enabling it; do not claim every message already goes to Claude.
- Shipment and other external lookup providers receive only the information needed for a requested lookup, such as a tracking number. The public website may use Cloudflare hosting/security and optional analytics under its own cookie choices.

Authorized UDC administrators and approved deal participants see only the information allowed for their role and the requested trade workflow. Verification files are not shared with counterparties or referring agents merely because they are involved in a deal. We do not sell personal information. Providers may process information outside India under applicable terms and law. Their own retention and processing terms also apply.

## Storage, retention and deletion
We intend to restrict sensitive documents to authorized access and short-lived links. No system offers absolute security. Production bucket settings still need a separate operational verification before UDC can make a verified private-storage claim.

A deletion request starts an identity-checked review, not instant or automatic erasure. Review covers account data, documents and extracted copies, messages and memories, provider copies and backups. UDC will explain what was removed and what remains, the reason and the applicable expiry. Some records may need to be retained for legal, accounting, fraud-prevention or dispute purposes. Backup/provider limits can delay complete removal. Closing a support ticket alone does not delete a file.

Category-specific retention periods and response deadlines require owner and lawyer confirmation and an operational process that meets them. The previous public policy's specific periods must not be carried forward as verified automated behaviour. Until this is settled, this draft is not ready for public release.

## Your choices
You can ask for access, correction, deletion or a privacy grievance through the contact above. Send "withdraw consent" in WhatsApp to stop further message, document and voice processing until you agree again; it does not itself erase existing records. Optional learning requires its own opt-in and cleaning of private details. Opting out stops future learning, not automatic removal of earlier records. Do not supply passwords or banking credentials to prove your identity.

UDC is for authorized business representatives aged 18 or older. Do not submit children's data. Material changes will be dated and communicated, with fresh consent where required.
